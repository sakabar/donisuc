# Donisuc

**Donisuc** (/ˈdɒnɪsʌk/) — A name derived from *dni* (Polish for "days"), *scope*, and *success*.

Donisuc is being developed as the successor to [Schronu](https://github.com/sakabar/Schronu), a personal task management tool.

Donisuc helps you foresee your day through recurring tasks and use the remaining time to advance your own projects.

## Motivation

Through using and improving Schronu, the concepts needed for task management have gradually become clearer. Incorporating these concepts into the existing system after the fact has made their relationships and the implementation structure complex. With Donisuc, we want to draw on this experience to rethink the necessary concepts and their relationships from the foundations of the design.

Our goal is to help users anticipate the time needed for daily routines, make plans that include buffers and sleep, and allocate time to the projects they want to advance. We also want to connect daily tasks to visions and goals so that users can decide what to prioritize. To support this, we will clarify the rules that determine the schedule and the properties the design can guarantee.

Individual issues, directions for improvement, and design considerations are documented in [Motivation and Directions for Improvement](docs/motivation.md).
